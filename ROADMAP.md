# Chirawn Roadmap

This document outlines the planned evolution of Chirawn, a personal entertainment hub for Ngọc Anh.

## Phase 1: Foundation & Real Data (Current)
*Goal: Remove placeholders and make the app feel "alive".*

- [x] **Streak System**: Implement logic for "Current Streak" based on daily game completions.
- [ ] **Streak Recovery**: Add the 48-hour restoration window and recovery UI.
- [ ] **Artwid Integration**: Introduce Artwid as the digital companion for greetings and support.
- [ ] **Detailed Stats**: Calculate total play time and best achievements for each game.
- [ ] **UI Refactoring**: Split the monolithic `ChirawnApp.kt` into feature-based modules.

## Phase 2: Personalization & Experience
*Goal: Make the app reflect the user's personal taste.*

- [ ] **Themes**: Support for Dynamic Color or curated "Calm & Modern" palettes.
- [ ] **Achievements**: Non-competitive badges for milestones (e.g., "Early Bird", "Persistence").
- [ ] **Contextual Greetings**: Dynamic messages based on time, mood, or activity.

## Phase 3: Digital Space Expansion
*Goal: Evolve beyond a simple game collection.*

- [ ] **Mini Journal**: A small space to record thoughts or mood after a game session.
- [ ] **Zen Games**: New calm games like Memory or Vietnamese Wordle.
- [ ] **Companion**: (Pending Approval) A gentle virtual pet responding to progress.
